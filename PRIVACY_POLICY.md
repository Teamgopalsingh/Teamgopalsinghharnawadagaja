## 2. INFORMATION COLLECTION & PERMISSIONS

We respect your privacy and collect only the minimum data necessary to provide functional community services.

### 2.1 Information You Provide Voluntarily
- **Account Registration Data:** Name, mobile phone number, and optional email address provided during user authentication.
- **Grievance & Issue Submissions:** Text descriptions, photo/document attachments, and category tags selected when submitting a local village complaint (e.g., street light malfunction, road repair, water supply disruption).
- **Feedback & Support Requests:** Communications submitted to our support team.

### 2.2 Device Permissions & Technical Data
The Android application requests specific runtime permissions solely for executing user-initiated features:

1. **Location Access (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`, `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`):**
   - **Purpose:** Used strictly to attach GPS coordinates to civic grievance reports submitted by residents in Harnawada Gaja.
   - **Operational Rule:** Location is fetched **only** when a user actively clicks "Attach Location" during complaint submission. We do **NOT** track, record, or store location data in the background or continuous tracking modes.
2. **Camera & Storage (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`):**
   - **Purpose:** Allows users to upload images of civic problems or relevant community documents. Access is restricted to user-selected media files under Android Scoped Storage guidelines.
3. **Phone Calls (`CALL_PHONE`):**
   - **Purpose:** Enables one-touch direct dialing to local emergency contacts, police station, health centers, or Panchayat helpline numbers listed in the app directory. Calls are initiated only upon explicit user tap.
4. **Push Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):**
   - **Purpose:** Delivers real-time community announcements, emergency weather/water alerts, and status updates regarding submitted complaints. Users may toggle notifications off at any time via system settings.

## 3. STRICT PROTECTION OF SENSITIVE PERSONAL DATA

### 3.1 Zero Tolerance for Political, Religious, or Caste Profiling
- **No Sensitive Profiling:** We strictly do **NOT** collect, request, process, track, or store data regarding political affiliation, voting preferences, electoral choices, caste identity, religious beliefs, or sensitive biometric/financial information.
- **Data Neutrality:** The platform operates strictly on civic development and community welfare criteria. No user data is ever analyzed or monetized for political campaigning or demographic profiling.

### 3.2 Data Security & Encryption
- All data transmissions between the mobile app and backend database servers are encrypted in transit using industry-standard **HTTPS / TLS 1.3** protocols.
- Database storage is hosted on secure cloud infrastructure (Supabase / PostgreSQL) protected by strict **Row-Level Security (RLS)** policies, ensuring users can access only their authorized submissions.

## 4. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

Collected information is processed exclusively for the following operational purposes:
1. **Civic Issue Resolution:** Categorizing and routing community grievances to local team volunteers and tracking resolution progress.
2. **Community Updates:** Publishing verified announcements regarding Gram Panchayat events, government scheme awareness camps, and public health drives in Harnawada Gaja.
3. **Account Security:** Verifying user phone numbers via One-Time Password (OTP) to prevent spam and automated bot submissions.
4. **App Optimization:** Analyzing aggregated, non-identifiable usage analytics to improve app performance and user interface responsiveness.

## 6. ACCOUNT DELETION WORKFLOW & DATA RETENTION

Users hold complete ownership over their personal data and possess the right to request full account and data erasure.

### 6.1 Account Deletion Request Methods
Users can initiate a complete account deletion request through either of the following two mechanisms:

1. **In-App Account Deletion Workflow:**
   - Open the App -> Navigate to **Profile & Settings** -> Tap **Account Management** -> Select **Delete My Account**.
   - Confirm via OTP verification to prevent unauthorized deletion.
2. **Web Deletion Portal:**
   - Visit the official deletion web request page at: [https://teamgopalsingh.org/delete-account](https://teamgopalsingh.org/delete-account)
   - Submit your registered mobile number and confirm the erasure request.

### 6.2 Data Purge Timelines & Policy
- Upon confirming an account deletion request, the user's account authentication record is immediately deactivated.
- All personally identifiable information (Name, Mobile Number, Email, Profile details) and user-submitted grievance attachments are permanently purged from active production databases within **30 calendar days**.
- De-identified, aggregated statistical data (e.g., total count of complaints resolved in Harnawada Gaja) may be retained for historical civic reporting.
